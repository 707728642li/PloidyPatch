# Natural hawthorn validation protocol v0.2

> Status update, 2026-08-07: this cohort was supplied from an unintended
> directory and is retired from the primary project benchmark. The completed
> artifacts are retained for provenance and engineering regression only. They
> will not be used for model selection, headline validation, or biological
> claims, and no further hawthorn analysis is planned.

Date frozen: 2026-08-07  
Target coordinate system: `Black_Primary`  
Server project: `/data/codexli/projects/PloidyPatch`

## Purpose

This arm tests whether plant-aware reconciliation can identify plausible
natural gene-model errors without using the evidence later used to validate
them. It is not a substitute for the controlled perturbation benchmarks and
does not establish a complete natural truth set.

## Discovery boundary

Candidate discovery uses 82,941 supplied Black_Hap1 and Black_Hap2 protein
records projected independently to the copied, checksum-verified
Black_Primary assembly with miniprot 0.18-r281. The frozen projection command
uses `-I -N4 --outn 4 --outs 0.8 --outc 0.5`; the run uses no RNA evidence.

`ploidypatch evidence discover-natural` then applies the following
predeclared filters:

- rank-1 projection only;
- identity and query coverage both at least 0.80;
- no reported frameshift or in-frame stop;
- existing-CDS overlap at most 0.10 for a missing-gene hypothesis;
- at least 30 bp of projected CDS extension for a boundary hypothesis;
- near-best mappings are any query alignments scoring at least 95% of that
  query's best alignment and are reported as an explicit multi-locus tier;
- two or more substantially overlapped target genes for a split-gene
  hypothesis.

The classifier distinguishes missing-gene, missing-internal-exon,
boundary-shift, split-gene, neutral alternative-CDS-structure, and ambiguous
opposite-strand coding-overlap hypotheses.
Nearby compatible projections are collapsed at the locus level while retaining
all query/model IDs and separate Hap1/Hap2 source support. RNA is not read by
this command, and every proposed edit remains `review_required`.

Multi-locus projection is not discarded because retained plant duplicates can
be the expected WGD/homeolog signal. It is instead separated from a unique
best-locus projection and cannot enter the highest RNA-supported tier. The
event graph also hard-abstains from a multi-locus repair unless independent
synteny disambiguates the intended copy; this preserves WGD candidates without
silently choosing a paralog or homeolog.

Fusion and copy-collapse calls are deliberately absent from this projection
classifier. A single target gene overlapped by several proteins is not enough
to distinguish a fusion from paralogy, isoforms, multi-mapping, or a collapsed
assembly. Those event types require source-gene topology plus independent
synteny, read-depth, or assembly evidence and remain hard constraints in the
event graph.

## Held-out RNA validation

Only after the discovery TSV and its manifest are frozen does
`ploidypatch evidence validate-natural-rna` verify their checksums and join
novel projected splice junctions to the independent Black-1/2/3 aggregate.
The primary threshold is at least two usable split reads in at least two of the
three samples. Candidate outcomes are:

- all novel junctions supported;
- some novel junctions supported;
- no qualifying junction observed; or
- not assessable because the hypothesis introduces no novel splice junction.

RNA non-observation is always missing evidence, never a contradiction. Read
counts are sums of per-junction observations, not unique molecules. Short-read
junction support validates local splicing only; it does not prove the full
transcript, translation, gene boundary, copy number, or gene grouping.

Non-primary RNA is joined only in a later, checksum-guarded analysis. The 57
files are reduced to 19 filename-stem groups; a junction is group-recurrent
only when at least two reads occur in at least two files per group and at
least two non-Black groups qualify. The stems have no verified biological
metadata, so recurrence is reported as context rather than matched validation,
does not change the primary tier, and never generates negative evidence.

## Correlation and truth limitations

Hap1 and Hap2 are phased assemblies from the same Black material, not
independent species. Their annotations were generated by closely related
pipelines and can share systematic errors. Thus “two haplotype sources” is a
useful consistency tier but not two independent biological replications.

The available RNA collection lacks read groups, verified tissue metadata, and
long-read/full-length transcript data. Black-1/2/3 are inferred from filenames
to be the closest matched replicates. Until independent Iso-Seq/full-length
cDNA or blinded manual review is added, reported loci must be called
RNA-supported hypotheses rather than corrected ground truth.

## Indexed assembly context

After RNA validation is frozen, `ploidypatch evidence
annotate-natural-assembly` verifies the validation checksum and queries the
uncompressed Black_Primary FASTA through its exact samtools FAI. For every
candidate it reports sequence length, distance to both sequence ends, observed
left/right flank, non-ACGT content, and lowercase soft-mask content in the
candidate interval and a predeclared +/-5 kb window.

Any non-ACGT base above the frozen zero-tolerance threshold, or a truncated
5-kb flank at a sequence edge, emits assembly-uncertainty evidence. A repair at
such a locus hard-abstains; a neutral alternative remains context-only.
Lowercase masking is recorded but remains context-only because no independent
repeat annotation or calibrated error rate is available. The scan is a local
sequence audit, not evidence that a contig is globally correct.

## Event-graph adapter

`ploidypatch evidence prepare-natural-graph` converts the frozen validation
table into the typed event-graph contract. Missing-gene, internal-exon,
boundary, and split hypotheses become repair states; alternative-CDS and
opposite-strand overlap hypotheses become neutral `uncertain` states whose
evidence is context-only.

Hap1 and Hap2 projections share one independent group, so their correlated
support cannot be counted twice. Projection reliability is conservatively set
to 0.69, matched short-read junction reliability to 0.80, and a multi-locus
near-best projection contributes an explicit mappability contradiction. RNA
non-observation emits no negative edge. These values are transparent
engineering priors, not calibrated probabilities; they must be calibrated on
development clades before publication-grade probability claims.

Under this policy, a projection without orthogonal structure evidence cannot
become an automatic natural repair. Fully supported novel junctions can raise
a candidate into graph review/acceptance, but the emitted annotation policy
remains `review_required` until a human-approved, reversible line edit exists.

## Frozen v0.2 result

The projection emitted 157,225 models. After rank, identity, coverage,
intactness, target-structure, and locus clustering filters, the RNA-blind
catalog contains 7,704 loci:

| Hypothesis | Loci |
|---|---:|
| missing annotation | 3,297 |
| missing internal exon | 127 |
| boundary shift | 507 |
| split gene | 175 |
| neutral alternative CDS structure | 3,589 |
| ambiguous opposite-strand coding overlap | 9 |

There are 2,914 loci supported by both haplotype sources. Held-out Black RNA
supports every novel projected junction for 452 loci and some novel junctions
for another 224; 5,168 have no novel junction that short reads can assess.

Adding projection specificity gives 80 loci in the strictest evidence tier:
two haplotype sources, a unique near-best target locus for every supporting
query, and all novel junctions supported by primary RNA. Twenty-one are
neutral alternative structures. The remaining 59 repair hypotheses comprise
34 missing annotations, 13 missing-internal-exon events, 11 boundary shifts,
and one split-gene event.

The constrained uncalibrated event graph reports 287 high-score repairs, 3,819
hard abstentions, and 3,598 neutral reviews. The 287 include single-haplotype
and partial-RNA tiers and must not be described as natural truth. The strict
59-locus subset is the priority manual/long-read review set. No annotation
patch was generated automatically.

The indexed assembly scan passes 7,683 of 7,704 candidates. Nineteen are within
5 kb of a sequence edge and two have non-ACGT bases in their flanking window;
no candidate interval itself contains a non-ACGT base, and the supplied FASTA
contains no lowercase soft mask at these windows. The 21 uncertain contexts
comprise 19 missing-annotation hypotheses and two neutral alternative-CDS
structures. All 19 repair hypotheses were already abstentions under the prior
multi-locus/structure constraints, while both neutral events were already
reviews. Consequently v0.3 changes zero decisions relative to v0.2. This is a
useful negative control: the context layer improves provenance without being
tuned to manufacture a performance change.

## Frozen secondary RNA result

All 57 non-primary BAMs completed without a non-empty error log. Their
per-sample tables and manifests contain 116 checksum entries, all verified
after relocation from the staging directory. Joining these samples with the
three primary Black samples yields 1,623,391 distinct genomic junction rows.
The grouped aggregation requires at least two reads in at least two files per
filename-stem group, then at least two qualifying non-Black groups. The 60
files form 20 groups in total: Black plus 19 non-primary filename stems.

Among all 7,704 candidates, 675 have every novel junction recurrent across
secondary groups and 281 have partial group recurrence. Another 1,580 have no
qualifying secondary-group junction, while 5,168 introduce no novel splice
junction and are therefore not assessable by this test. The last two outcomes
are missing evidence and do not contradict homology or primary RNA evidence.

The most useful predeclared check is replication of the frozen strict primary
tier. Seventy-seven of its 80 loci reproduce every tested novel junction in at
least two non-Black groups; three have no group-recurrent junction observed.
This is strong local splice corroboration, but it is not a 77/80 estimate of
full gene-model accuracy: samples can share mapping and preparation biases,
filename stems are not verified tissues or genotypes, and junctions do not
validate translation, distant transcript connectivity, copy number, or gene
grouping. Consequently the secondary result changes neither the primary tier
nor the `review_required` patch policy.

The assembly-preserving v0.2 join feeds all 956 candidates with at least one
group-recurrent novel junction into event-graph v0.4 as `context` edges. These
edges retain a separate non-Black filename-group provenance, reliability 0.60,
and zero score contribution. All 21 prior assembly-uncertainty edges remain in
the graph. A candidate-by-candidate comparison against v0.3 verifies 7,704
matched IDs and zero decision changes: 287 high-score accepts, 3,819 hard
abstentions, and 3,598 reviews. This deliberately tests evidence integration
without turning post-discovery replication into a tuned confidence boost.

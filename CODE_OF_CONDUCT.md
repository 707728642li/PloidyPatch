# Code of conduct

PloidyPatch is committed to a respectful, inclusive, and scientifically
constructive community.

Participants must communicate professionally, distinguish evidence from
opinion, respect data and authorship boundaries, and avoid harassment,
discrimination, personal attacks, or the disclosure of private information.
Project maintainers may edit, reject, or remove contributions that violate
these expectations and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation.

Concerns may be reported privately to
[litaishan@caf.ac.cn](mailto:litaishan@caf.ac.cn). Reports will be handled as
confidentially as reasonably possible.
